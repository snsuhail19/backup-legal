BACKUP — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BACKUP ("Bot"), operated by ZEXA CORPORATION, may process information when used in Discord servers.

1. Information We May Process

Depending on the features enabled by a server, the Bot may process Discord-provided identifiers and server information such as user ID, username/display name, server ID, channel ID, role ID, and message-related information needed to perform commands.

2. Configuration Data

Server configuration data may be stored so features continue to work after the Bot restarts. This can include welcome-channel settings, auto-role settings, and ticket-related configuration.

3. Moderation Data

If moderation features that store records are enabled, the Bot may store information necessary to provide those features, such as user identifiers, moderator identifiers, reasons, and timestamps.

4. How Information Is Used

Information is used only as reasonably necessary to operate, maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the Bot's features.

5. Sharing

We do not intentionally sell personal information. Information may be processed by hosting or infrastructure providers when necessary to operate the Bot, subject to their applicable terms and policies.

6. Data Retention

Configuration and operational data may be retained while needed for the Bot's features. Retention periods can vary by feature and may change as the Bot develops.

7. Data Requests

If you have a question about data associated with your Discord server or account, contact the Bot's owner or support team through the official support method provided by the Bot's Discord server.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information used by the Bot, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

The Bot is intended to be used in accordance with Discord's age requirements and applicable law.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Bot's features or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.

This Privacy Policy is a general template and is not legal advice. Review it and adjust it to match your Bot's actual data storage and processing before publishing.
